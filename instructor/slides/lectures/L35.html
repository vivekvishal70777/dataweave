Q30. How do you work with dates and periods?

  • Date literals use pipes: |2026-08-20|, |P7D| (ISO-8601 periods). Prefer injecting time as a field (Lab 28) over now() in reusable modules.
  • Timezones: DateTime has an offset; LocalDateTime does not. Shift with payload.ts as DateTime >> "Asia/Kolkata".
  • More helpers: dw::core::Dates (daysBetween, atBeginningOfDay) — Q66–Q67.
